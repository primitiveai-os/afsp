## Individual CLA signatories

Individual signatures are recorded automatically when contributors accept the Individual CLA on their first pull request.

## How to sign

- **Individuals:** open a pull request. A bot will post a link to accept the [Individual CLA](INDIVIDUAL.md).
- **Organizations:** follow the steps in the [Corporate CLA](CORPORATE.md).
