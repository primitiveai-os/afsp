# AFSP Corporate Contributor License Agreement

This Agreement lets an organization authorize its employees or contractors to contribute to the Agentic Financial Services Protocol ("AFSP") project (the "Project") on its behalf. The Project is currently maintained by CIDR Technologies Inc d.b.a Primitive ("Primitive"). The organization keeps ownership of its Contributions; this Agreement grants licenses only.

Each authorized individual must also accept the [Individual CLA](INDIVIDUAL.md).

## How to sign

1. Open a GitHub issue using the [Corporate CLA template](https://github.com/primitiveai-os/afsp/issues/new?template=corporate-cla.yml), titled "Corporate CLA: [Organization name]".
2. Provide the organization's legal name, address, point of contact, the list of authorized contributors with their GitHub usernames, and the name, title and date of the person accepting this Agreement for the organization.
3. A maintainer reviews the issue, applies the `cla-signed` label, and adds the organization to [SIGNATORIES.md](SIGNATORIES.md).

To add or remove authorized contributors later, comment on the original issue.
